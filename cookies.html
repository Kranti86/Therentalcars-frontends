Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Therentalcars uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. This policy explains what they do and how you control them.

Categories

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site and checkout to work (e.g. your session, your cookie choice). Always on.
  • Analytics — Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity, to understand usage and improve the site. On by default; you can reject them.
  • Advertising — Google Ads, to measure campaigns and show relevant ads. On by default; you can reject it.

Your choices

When you first visit, our cookie banner lets you keep or reject non-essential cookies. Analytics and advertising cookies are on by default; click Reject on the banner to turn them off (we remember your choice and pass it to Google via Consent Mode). You can change your choice any time by clearing the site's cookies or using the banner link.

Third parties

Google (Analytics/Ads/Tag Manager), Microsoft (Clarity), and PayPal set their own cookies under their respective policies.
