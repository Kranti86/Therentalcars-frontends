About Us

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Therentalcars is an online car-rental comparison and booking service operated by Himalayan Haze LLC ("Therentalcars", "we", "us"). We help you compare live prices from third-party car-rental suppliers and reserve online. We are a booking intermediary — we are not the rental company and not an insurer. The rental agreement is between you and the supplier; any insurance is provided by a third-party insurer.

What we do

Therentalcars scans live prices from car-rental suppliers and surfaces your best match, so you can compare and reserve online in seconds — cheapest first, closest next — without phone calls. You pay a small platform fee (and optional coverage) online and settle the rental at the counter.

Who we are

Therentalcars is operated by Himalayan Haze Technologies (OPC) Private Limited. We're a small team focused on making car rental fast, transparent, and fair.
