Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Therentalcars is an online car-rental comparison and booking service operated by Himalayan Haze LLC ("Therentalcars", "we", "us"). We help you compare live prices from third-party car-rental suppliers and reserve online. We are a booking intermediary — we are not the rental company and not an insurer. The rental agreement is between you and the supplier; any insurance is provided by a third-party insurer.

Information we collect

When you reserve, we collect your name, email, phone number, and trip details (location, dates, vehicle). Payments are processed by Payment processor e.g- Stripe,Paypal,Razorpay and others. — we do not store your full card number. We also collect basic usage data (device, pages, approximate location) via analytics.

How we use it

To create and manage your reservation, process payment, send confirmations and service messages, provide support, prevent fraud, comply with law, and improve the service.

Who we share it with

  • Car-rental suppliers — to fulfill your booking.
  • PayPal — to authorize and capture payment.
  • Insurance partner — if you buy coverage, to issue your certificate.
  • Service providers — Google (Analytics, Ads), Microsoft Clarity, Mapbox, and our email/hosting providers, under their terms.
  • Advertising partners (Google) — if you make a reservation, we may share your email address and phone number with Google in hashed (encrypted) form to measure our ads and to show you, or stop showing you, relevant ads ("Customer Match"). Google matches it to its own accounts and does not use it for anything else. If you click Reject on our cookie banner before booking, we never include you; you can also ask us to remove you at any time (see Contact).

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy. You control non-essential cookies via our consent banner.

Data retention & security

We keep reservation data as long as needed for the booking, legal, and accounting purposes, then delete or anonymize it. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards; no method is 100% secure.

Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents may request to know, delete, or correct personal information we hold, and to opt out of "sharing" for cross-context advertising. We do not sell personal information. To exercise these rights, contact carrental@reservationdes.online. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

Children

The service is intended for adults (21+) and is not directed to children.
